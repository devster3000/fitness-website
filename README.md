# fitness-website
Summer project - A website made entirely with HTML and CSS, centered around a fictional fitness company named TOKA.

## The software development company that you work for has secured an opportunity to pitch for a new contract with ToKa Fitness.
### ToKa Fitness currently provides customers with:
    • Personal training sessions
    • Advice about fitness training
    • Advice about healthy living
### The client would like a digital solution that:
    • Provide information and advice about fitness training and healthy living
    • Provide access to digital content that supports training and healthy lifestyles
    • Encourage customers to engage with more ToKa Fitness services
### Following market research, customers requested:
    • Free and paid-for content
    • Accessibility features for users with sight loss
    • Social features
    • Customisable workout plans
    • Customisable meal plans
